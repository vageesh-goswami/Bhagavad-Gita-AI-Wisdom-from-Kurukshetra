[BG 2.47] Topic: action, duty, results, exams, interviews, detachment
Sanskrit:
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥
Hindi meaning:
मनुष्य का अधिकार अपने कर्म पर है, उसके निश्चित परिणाम पर नहीं। फल की चिंता को कर्म का एकमात्र कारण न बनाओ और निष्क्रियता से भी आसक्त न होओ।
English meaning:
Your responsibility is to act well; the final result is not fully under your control. Do not make reward the sole motive for action, and do not use uncertainty as a reason for inaction.
Practical themes: Prepare sincerely, focus on controllable actions, measure effort and learning, and release anxiety about outcomes.

[BG 2.48] Topic: balance, success, failure, performance pressure, calm action
Sanskrit:
योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनञ्जय।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते॥
Hindi meaning:
हे अर्जुन, आसक्ति छोड़कर संतुलित मन से अपना कर्म करो। सफलता और असफलता में समभाव रखना ही योग कहा गया है।
English meaning:
Act from a steady mind without clinging to success. Equanimity in success and failure is described as yoga.
Practical themes: Before an interview or examination, return attention to the next useful action rather than repeatedly predicting the outcome.

[BG 2.14] Topic: discomfort, resilience, temporary emotions, anxiety
Sanskrit:
मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः।
आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत॥
Hindi meaning:
इन्द्रियों के संपर्क से सर्दी-गर्मी और सुख-दुःख उत्पन्न होते हैं। वे आते-जाते और अस्थायी हैं; धैर्यपूर्वक उनका सामना करो।
English meaning:
Pleasure, pain, heat, and cold arise through changing conditions. They come and go, so meet them with patience.
Practical themes: Strong emotions are temporary signals, not permanent facts. Pause, breathe, and avoid making an impulsive decision at the emotional peak.

[BG 2.20] Topic: soul, death, identity, grief, impermanence
Sanskrit:
न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः।
अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे॥
Hindi meaning:
आत्मा न जन्म लेती है और न मरती है। वह अजन्मा, नित्य और शाश्वत है; शरीर के नष्ट होने पर भी उसका नाश नहीं होता।
English meaning:
The self is described as unborn, eternal, and enduring; it is not destroyed when the body is destroyed.
Practical themes: The verse distinguishes deeper identity from temporary bodily and external change, and is traditionally discussed in the context of grief and mortality.

[BG 3.35] Topic: own path, comparison, career, duty, authenticity
Sanskrit:
श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात्।
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः॥
Hindi meaning:
दूसरे के मार्ग को अच्छी तरह अपनाने से अपना कर्तव्य अपूर्ण रूप से करना भी श्रेष्ठ है। अपने स्वभाव और उत्तरदायित्व के अनुरूप मार्ग अधिक सुरक्षित है।
English meaning:
It is better to follow one's own duty imperfectly than to imitate another person's path perfectly.
Practical themes: Learn from others without copying their life blindly. Choose work that matches your responsibilities, strengths, and values.

[BG 4.7–4.8] Topic: dharma, justice, restoration, leadership
Sanskrit:
यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत।
अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदात्मानं सृजाम्यहम्॥
परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम्।
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे॥
Hindi meaning:
जब-जब धर्म की हानि और अधर्म की वृद्धि होती है, तब धर्म की पुनर्स्थापना, सज्जनों की रक्षा और अन्याय के प्रतिरोध के लिए दिव्य हस्तक्षेप प्रकट होता है।
English meaning:
When righteousness declines and injustice rises, the teaching describes divine manifestation for protection, correction, and restoration of dharma.
Practical themes: Leadership means protecting what is right, correcting harmful systems, and acting when passivity would allow injustice to grow.

[BG 6.5] Topic: self-improvement, discipline, confidence, personal responsibility
Sanskrit:
उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत्।
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः॥
Hindi meaning:
मनुष्य को अपने द्वारा अपना उत्थान करना चाहिए, स्वयं को गिराना नहीं चाहिए। अनुशासित मन मित्र बनता है और अनियंत्रित मन शत्रु जैसा कार्य कर सकता है।
English meaning:
Lift yourself through your own disciplined effort; do not degrade yourself. The trained mind can be an ally, while the untrained mind can work against you.
Practical themes: Build small repeatable habits, reduce self-defeating behaviour, and use compassionate self-discipline rather than shame.

[BG 6.26] Topic: focus, distraction, meditation, attention, study
Sanskrit:
यतो यतो निश्चरति मनश्चञ्चलमस्थिरम्।
ततस्ततो नियम्यैतदात्मन्येव वशं नयेत्॥
Hindi meaning:
चंचल मन जहाँ-जहाँ भटके, वहाँ-वहाँ से उसे धैर्यपूर्वक वापस लाकर नियंत्रित और केंद्रित करना चाहिए।
English meaning:
Whenever the restless mind wanders, gently bring it back under steady awareness.
Practical themes: Concentration is not never getting distracted; it is repeatedly noticing distraction and returning to the chosen task.

[BG 12.13–12.14] Topic: compassion, humility, emotional maturity, devotion
Sanskrit:
अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च।
निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी॥
सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः॥
Hindi meaning:
जो किसी से द्वेष नहीं करता, सबके प्रति मैत्री और करुणा रखता है, अहंकार से मुक्त, सुख-दुःख में संतुलित, क्षमाशील, संयमी और दृढ़ है—वह प्रिय भक्त है।
English meaning:
A valued devotee is described as free from hatred, friendly, compassionate, humble, forgiving, self-controlled, and steady in pleasure and pain.
Practical themes: Strength can coexist with kindness. Practise clear boundaries without hatred, and disagree without dehumanising another person.

[BG 16.21] Topic: desire, anger, greed, self-control, harmful habits
Sanskrit:
त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः।
कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत्॥
Hindi meaning:
अनियंत्रित कामना, क्रोध और लोभ आत्मविनाश की ओर ले जाने वाले तीन द्वार बताए गए हैं; इसलिए इनसे सावधान रहना चाहिए।
English meaning:
Uncontrolled desire, anger, and greed are described as three gates leading toward self-destruction and should therefore be restrained.
Practical themes: Notice the trigger, delay reaction, remove easy access to the harmful action, and seek support when a pattern is difficult to control alone.

[BG 18.47] Topic: profession, vocation, strengths, responsibility
Sanskrit:
श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात्।
स्वभावनियतं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम्॥
Hindi meaning:
अपने स्वभाव से जुड़े कर्तव्य को अपूर्ण रूप से करना भी दूसरे के कर्तव्य को उत्तम रूप से करने से श्रेष्ठ है।
English meaning:
Work arising from one's own nature is preferable, even when performed imperfectly, to perfectly imitating another person's work.
Practical themes: Career decisions should consider aptitude, responsibility, values, and long-term fit—not prestige or comparison alone.

[BG 18.66] Topic: surrender, trust, guilt, spiritual refuge
Sanskrit:
सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज।
अहं त्वां सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा शुचः॥
Hindi meaning:
सभी मानसिक सहारों और अहंकारपूर्ण नियंत्रण को छोड़कर परम आश्रय में आओ; भय और शोक मत करो।
English meaning:
The verse calls for complete refuge in the Divine and offers freedom from fear, guilt, and sorrow.
Practical themes: After sincere effort and ethical reflection, accept what cannot be controlled. Spiritual surrender is not avoidance of responsibility.
